Hi, I'm Freddy. I'm a third-year CS PhD student at Princeton. I'm interested in algorithmic game theory - analyzing algorithms for settings where agents have their own incentives. I am fortunate to be advised by [Matt Weinberg](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/~smattw/).
